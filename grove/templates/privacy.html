Privacy

{{ SITE_NAME }} and the {{ SITE_NAME }} Chrome extension don't ask for an account and don't collect personal information.

  • Your name, layout and widget choices are saved in your browser's local storage. That data never leaves your browser, and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Weather, news and other widget data is fetched by our server from public sources. Your browser only talks to {{ SITE_NAME }}.
  • The Chrome extension only replaces your new tab page with {{ SITE_NAME }}. It asks for no permissions and can't read your browsing history or other tabs.
  • Our host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for a short time to keep the site running. We don't sell or share them, and we don't use ads or tracking cookies.

If you submit a campus photo, we use your name only to credit the photo.

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
