About {{ SITE_NAME }}

{{ SITE_NAME }} puts your Ole Miss day on one page: a campus photo of the day, Rebels game day, your next class, what's open for dining, what's on tonight in Oxford, weather, countdowns and campus news.

  • Click your name in the greeting to change it.
  • Settings lets you pick Student, Fan or Alum, add your class CRNs, choose widgets and columns. Arrange lets you drag widgets around.
  • Study mode hides everything except a big clock and a focus timer.
  • Press / to search. Start with om to search only olemiss.edu.

Your name, layout, CRNs and countdowns are saved only in this browser.

Share your campus photo

Every day's background is a photo of Ole Miss or Oxford taken by a student or fan, with their name on it. The Grove in the fall, the Lyceum at night, the Square, game day: send us your best shot. Only send photos you took yourself.
